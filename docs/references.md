# Official reference register

- IBM: https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/ui-icons/design/ . Grid, padding, stroke, corners and optical balance.

- Spotify: https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design . Integration branding: symbol/logo distinctions and digital/print minima. Not a full corporate manual.
- Slack: https://slack.com/media-kit . Official entry point for logo files, product screenshots and brand center.
- SeatGeek: https://brand.seatgeek.com/ . Structural inspiration only in this release; detailed current rules not independently re-extracted.
